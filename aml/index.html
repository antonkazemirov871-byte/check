❓ FAQ

What is AML verification? ⌄

It is an assessment of a wallet's exposure to flagged, sanctioned or high-risk counterparties.

How is the risk score calculated? ⌄

The score aggregates transaction activity, counterparties and exposure ratio into a 0–100 value.

What blockchain data is analyzed? ⌄

Public on-chain data: transfers, counterparties and token holdings of the address.
